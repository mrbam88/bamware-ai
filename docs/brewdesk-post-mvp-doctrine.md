# BrewDesk post-MVP doctrine (CEO, 2026-10-05)

MVP is **in the App Store**. That was the hard gate. Work is not done — it **starts**.

## Priority order (binding)

### 1. Product truth (Engineering — number one)
The app must **work** on spot-check and when referred to a friend.

- API does what we claim (find cafes for laptop work).
- **Rankings we can swear by** — if Bilal spot-checks a cafe, the score/rank should feel right.
- Bad filters, hollow pins, junk non-cafe first results = **P0**, not polish.

Everything else yields to this until weekly spot-checks pass.

### 2. UI/UX never settle (Engineering — number two)
Continuous self-improvement. No complacency after ship. Secondary to ranking truth.

### 3. Marketing and product in harmony
Bilal is **conductor**. Marketing drives acquisition; Engineering keeps product proud.

- Marketing is a trust domain (Bilal leads eng more naturally than marketing).
- No campaign that outruns a broken ranking experience.
- Shared weekly: what we are proud to show vs not ready to amplify.

## Roles

| Role | Owns |
|---|---|
| CEO | Direction, spend, store, human consoles; conducts |
| Chief of Staff | Unblock, cadence, surface risks early |
| Engineering Lead | Ranking/API truth, quality bar, sprint tech plan |
| Delivery lead | Pull backlog into sprint; factory light; no idle with open P0s |
| Marketing agent | Brand/social strategy and prep; CEO approval to publish |

## Factory rule
If rankings are wrong, builders work ranking/API — not random polish.
