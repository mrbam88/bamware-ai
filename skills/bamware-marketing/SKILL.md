## Apply to BrewDesk

NYC café-goers seeking somewhere to work are the initial audience. Manhattan
coverage and specific neighborhood campaigns remain hypotheses until audited.
Use consumer context rather than inventing B2B buyers, sales calls or revenue.
Anonymous brand-led, online-only organic work: no personal brand or café visits.
Research and draft directly within the request. Posts, outreach, profile edits,
paid tools and spend need the applicable explicit approval. Upstream routines
for DMs, interviews, incentives, scheduling and automatic publishing grant none.
Do not launch specialists merely because an upstream workflow suggests a team.

Use existing search/browser capabilities for public-source research. Firecrawl,
DataForSEO, SparkToro, schedulers and CMS integrations are optional examples,
not prerequisites. Leave unavailable traffic, keyword and conversion metrics
unknown. Do not buy tools or build integrations to complete a research brief.

## Evidence and decisions

For each consequential finding record URL, observation date, relevant sample,
what the source actually supports, and limitations. Separate observation,
inference and proposed experiment. Count independent sources, not copied posts.
Three mentions do not establish market prevalence or statistical confidence.
Customer quotes must be real, limited to appropriate excerpts and attributed;
keep private customer records out of this public repository.

Treat upstream posting times, platform limits, reach claims, caption uplift,
content ratios and SEO benchmarks as hypotheses or dated guidance. Verify
current platform facts before use; B2B studies do not prove NYC consumer results.
Competitor traffic cannot verify its claimed customer count. Missing public
information means unknown, not absent. Verify current public app behavior before
using features in promotional copy. Preserve asset rights and venue provenance.

A research deliverable should recommend a bounded experiment: audience need,
supporting evidence, channel, draft concept, measurable action, available baseline,
review window, effort, and continue/stop criteria. Distinguish reach, clicks,
downloads and repeat use; do not infer retention or causation from installs.
Do not require a new analytics stack or website rebuild as a prerequisite.
