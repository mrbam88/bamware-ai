# References — policy

Contact details are deliberately NOT stored in git.

## Standard answers

- "References" field → **"Available on request."**
- "May we contact your former employers?" → **Yes.**
- Named references → only when hard-required or post-offer.

## Rules

- Never supply a name, email, or phone for a reference without asking Bilal first.
- Always give Bilal a heads-up before any company contacts a reference.
- If a form blocks submission without named references, STOP and hand it to Bilal.
